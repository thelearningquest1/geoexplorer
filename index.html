Welcome to GeoExplorer!

The ultimate interactive world map for curious minds. Explore every country, paint your own map, discover amazing facts, and test your geography knowledge!

  • 🗺️
    A real, detailed world map with every country and their states/provinces
  • 🎨
    Paint countries and states with any colour you choose
  • 📚
    Click any country to see capitals, populations, landmarks, fun facts and more
  • 🧩
    Test yourself with the geography quiz — can you find every country?
🔭

Navigating the Map

Use these controls to explore the world:

  • 🖱️
    Scroll wheel or pinch to zoom in and out
  • ✋
    Toggle Pan Mode (top right of map) to drag the map around
  • 🌍
    Use the continent buttons on the left to jump to any region instantly
  • 🔍
    Use the search bar in the panel to find any country quickly
  • +−
    Use the + and − buttons on the map to zoom precisely
🎨

Painting the Map

Make the map your own with colours!

  • 🎨
    Pick a colour from the colour palette in the toolbar
  • 🖱️
    Click any country to paint it — or switch to States Mode to paint individual states and provinces!
  • 📋
    The legend on the map tracks your colour groups — great for making themed maps!
  • 🗑️
    Hit Clear All in the toolbar to start fresh at any time
📚

Exploring Countries

  • 🏛️

  • 🗺️

  • ⭐
    Read a cool fun fact about every single country!
